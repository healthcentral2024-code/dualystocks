---
name: HeyGen cost control
description: Cost discipline for avatar-video work that uses HeyGen.
---

Treat HeyGen generations as an expensive final step. Validate framing, clean plates, transcript, audio processing, and catalog voice previews without generating paid video whenever possible.

**Why:** The user explicitly wants HeyGen usage minimized and does not want repeated paid experiments.

**How to apply:** Use free previews or static-avatar composites first. Ask for explicit approval before a paid voice/video test and again before a long final generation if the scope or voice changes.

For DualyStocks videos, the approved direction is the user's horizontal seated avatar at the table, cropped to retain the seated posture, with the official Kurt voice speaking Spanish.

**Why:** The user rejected public avatars and two closer portraits, then explicitly approved the seated custom avatar after a short composite test.

**How to apply:** Reuse this avatar-and-voice pairing for related edits unless the user asks for a new direction. Still deliver a short composite test before any long generation when the source video or framing changes.
